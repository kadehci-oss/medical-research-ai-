## 7. Tedavi Etkinliği ve Güvenlilik
- **Thomas Khoo ve Hector Chinoy** (PMID: [37884200](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37884200/)): The discovery of autoantibodies directed against the 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-CoA reductase (HMGCR) enzyme has defined a sub-set of immune-mediated necrotising myopathy (IMNM) which is strongly associated with exposure to statin medications. Conflicting data exists on whether malignancies are associated with anti-HMGCR and further clarification is required to determine the degree of cancer screening required.
- **Payam Mohassel ve Andrew L Mammen** (PMID: [29480216](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29480216/)): Anti-HMGCR myopathy was first recognized and characterized in patients with a history of statin exposure and immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy.
- **Joome Suh ve Anthony A Amato** (PMID: [38801022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38801022/)): The immune-mediated necrotizing myopathies (IMNM) are autoimmune myositides clinically characterized by proximal predominant weakness and elevated creatine kinase (CK). They may be associated with autoantibodies (anti-HMGCR, anti-SRP), triggered by statin use (e.
- **Yves Allenbach et al.** (PMID: [33093664](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33093664/)): Most IMNMs are associated with anti-signal recognition particle (anti-SRP) or anti-3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-coA reductase (anti-HMGCR) myositis-specific autoantibodies, although ~20% of patients with IMNM remain seronegative. IMNMs are frequently rapidly progressive and severe, displaying high serum creatine kinase levels, and failure to treat IMNMs effectively may lead to severe muscle impairment.
- **Payam Mohassel ve Andrew L Mammen** (PMID: [23519993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23519993/)): However, these drugs can also be associated with muscle symptoms ranging from mild myalgias to severe rhabdomyolysis. Although statin myotoxicity is usually self-limited, in some instances statin-exposed subjects can develop an autoimmune myopathy typically characterized by progressive weakness, muscle enzyme elevations, a necrotizing myopathy on muscle biopsy, and autoantibodies that recognize 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-coenzyme A reductase (HMGCR), the pharmacologic target of statins.
